The native small-GEMM code is a local Windows HIP 5.7 workaround inspired by:
https://github.com/Schaka/rocm-gfx803/blob/8fdcf7b1305d0cfc6fc8c1fc7b1bfb5f73494e78/patches/rocblas/small-gemm-assembly-miscompute.patch
It is not a direct application of that Linux source patch to Windows.
The native source files are copies of the source-machine implementation.
The portable build/test helpers are adaptations prepared for this handoff.
Historical numeric results are not a fresh target-machine validation.

Original backend is acquired separately from its publisher:
https://github.com/advanced-lvl-up/Rx470-Vega10-Rx580-gfx803-gfx900-fix-AMD-GPU/releases/tag/v1.0.0
